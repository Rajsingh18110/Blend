# About Blend

**Blend** is a next-generation privacy-first search engine designed for modern researchers, developers, and professionals who demand both speed and intelligence. Built with a focus on user privacy, Blend combines distributed search, AI-powered insights, and deep content enrichment into one seamless experience.

## Core Philosophy

We believe that search should be:

- **Private by default** — No tracking, no profiling, no data selling
- **Fast and intelligent** — Instant results without sacrificing quality
- **Context-aware** — Understanding what you're really looking for
- **Multilingual** — Native support for Hindi, English, and 50+ languages
- **Open source** — Transparent, auditable, community-driven

## What Makes Blend Different

### Intelligent Ranking
Blend uses a proprietary ranking algorithm that combines:
- **Domain authority scoring** — Wikipedia, GitHub, and trusted sources get priority
- **Relevance signals** — TF-IDF weighted keywords with exact phrase bonuses
- **Engine diversity** — Results from blend native, DuckDuckGo, Brave, and Wikipedia engines
- **Spam detection** — Automatically filters clickbait and low-quality content

### AI-Powered Intelligence
Integrated with **Sarvam 2B**, an Indian multilingual LLM, Blend can:
- Generate intelligent summaries of search results
- Understand Hindi and regional language queries
- Provide entity-style knowledge cards
- Expand searches automatically for better recall

### Deep Content Enrichment
The Blend Crawler:
- Fetches and analyzes actual page content
- Extracts metadata, descriptions, and favicons
- Enriches results with better descriptions
- Prioritizes quality over quantity

## Search Modes

### ⚡ Fast Response
Lightweight, instant searches perfect for quick information lookups. No AI processing, pure speed.

### 🔍 Deep Research
Crawler-assisted search with enriched results, source verification, and detailed content analysis. Perfect for thorough investigations.

### 🧠 AI Answer
AI-powered guided responses with knowledge cards, structured answers, and intelligent recommendations. Ideal for learning and exploration.

## Technology Stack

**Backend:** Python Flask + async Scrapy-inspired crawler  
**AI Engine:** Sarvam 2B (multilingual)  
**Search Engines:** Native + 12 distributed engines  
**Database:** Distributed caching with Valkey  
**Protocol:** Apache-2.0
